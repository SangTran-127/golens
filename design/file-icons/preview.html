GoLens file-type icons

46 custom symbols. Identity tints by family (Go, infra, data, web), the theme accent for folders and neutral for docs and tooling. Gopher-derived glyph after the Go gopher by Renée French (CC BY 4.0). Other technology glyphs are original drawings that only evoke the technology; all trademarks belong to their owners.

Full set, light: 16 px tinted / mono, 32 px tinted / mono / hierarchical
