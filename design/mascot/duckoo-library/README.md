# GoLens Duckoo-Inspired Go Gopher Library

Bo suu tap Go Gopher 2D hand-illustrated comic-ink & cel-shaded phat trien theo phong cach **POP MART Duckoo**, tuan thu ty le goc **Go Gopher (Renee French, CC BY 4.0)** va da qua kiem duyet giai phau 8 cong.

- **Visual Browser**: Mo `index.html` de duyet theo nhom, tim kiem nhanh, phong to xem chi tiet va sao chep duong dan file.
- **Structured Index**: `catalog.json` luu day du metadata cho script hoac tool tu dong.

## Tong quan thu vien

| Thu muc | Mo ta | So luong |
|---|---|---|
| `characters/` | 18 full-body Go Gopher collectibles across Series 01 and Series 02 | 18 |
| `icons/` | 10 cosplay & festival app icon concepts plus the native 1024px Boba Milk Tea master | 11 |
| `badges/` | 8 mini-diorama scene badges plus 5 circular Welcome medallions (@2x) | 13 |
| `worlds/` | 5 wide IDE & DMG installer worldbuilding scenes with multi-Gopher casts | 5 |
| `boards/` | 9 full-resolution multi-panel sheets and standalone master artwork | 9 |
| **Tong cong** | **Toan bo bo Duckoo-inspired Go Gopher** | **56** |
